# Project banner image prompts

Banners live in `public/projects/<slug>.png`, are referenced from each project's
frontmatter `image:` field, and render inside a 16:9 `aspect-video` frame with
`object-cover`. Keep every banner on the same visual system — the set is read as
one row of cards on the home page, so a single off-style image is very visible.

Target size: **1656 × 950** (or any clean 16:9 ≥ 1600 wide).

## Shared style block

Prepend this to every project prompt:

> Soft 3D isometric claymorphic illustration, matte clay and porcelain surfaces
> with gently rounded bevels. Seamless warm cream off-white background (#F7F2E8),
> flat and untextured. Primary accent is a muted sage green; secondary accents are
> pastel blush pink, soft lavender, powder blue and mint — used sparingly. One large
> rounded-corner screen floats slightly at the centre showing a simplified abstract
> UI. Smaller objects are arranged around it, each resting on its own small rounded
> pastel pedestal tile. Thin dashed sage-green connector lines with small glowing dot
> nodes link the elements. Soft diffuse shadows, even light, no harsh highlights or
> reflections. Centred, balanced composition with generous negative space; nothing
> cropped by the frame edges. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos or UI
> labels anywhere in the image. Wide 16:9 aspect ratio.

## Rabbehni — loyalty SaaS

> Subject: a customer-loyalty platform. At the centre, a floating rounded tablet
> showing an abstract merchant analytics dashboard — a sage-green donut chart, a
> small bar series and a rising line, all wordless. Around it on pastel pedestals:
> a standing QR-code plaque on a café counter stand, a smartphone displaying a
> loyalty stamp card with green filled dots, a small clay storefront shop building
> with an awning, a wrapped gift box, a stack of round reward tokens, a small prize
> wheel divided into pastel segments, and a globe with a single map pin. Dashed green
> lines flow from the QR plaque and phone toward the central dashboard.

## Piazza Napoletana — restaurant reservations

> Subject: an online restaurant table-reservation system. At the centre, a floating
> rounded tablet showing an abstract month calendar grid with several sage-green
> highlighted day cells and a column of time-slot pills, all wordless. Around it on
> pastel pedestals: a small round restaurant table with two chairs and a plate, a
> wall clock, a folded reserved-table tent card, a clay Italian trattoria facade with
> a striped awning, a wine glass, and a map pin. Dashed green lines run from the
> table and clock into the central calendar. Warm and inviting, slightly Italian in
> feel but with no flags or text.

## Mo7amy — lawyer marketplace (in progress)

> Subject: an online marketplace for booking legal consultations. At the centre, a
> floating rounded tablet showing an abstract lawyer-search result list — stacked
> pastel profile cards each with a round avatar placeholder, a row of small sage-green
> rating dots and a green "available" pill, all wordless. Around it on pastel
> pedestals: a set of brass scales of justice, a judge's gavel resting on its block,
> a small stack of closed law books, a weekly calendar tile with a few sage-green
> booked cells, a shield badge with a check mark for verified lawyers, a video-call
> bubble icon, and a map pin. Dashed green lines run from the scales and the calendar
> into the central search screen. Calm, trustworthy and professional in mood.

## Hiya — single-store e-commerce

> Subject: an online store with inventory management. At the centre, a floating
> rounded tablet showing an abstract product-grid storefront — rows of pastel product
> thumbnail cards with a sage-green filter sidebar, all wordless. Around it on pastel
> pedestals: a stack of shipping cartons, a fanned colour-swatch palette, a hanging
> size tag label, a shopping bag, a small pile of banknotes with a coin for
> cash-on-delivery, a sheet of paper representing an invoice, and a small hourglass.
> Dashed green lines connect the cartons and swatches to the central storefront
> screen. Clean, premium retail feel.
